# Credits

## pixelfx

Explosion and impact effects in `public/assets/fx.png` are rendered at build time by `tools/fx/bake.mjs` from
[pixelfx](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx) (commit 5294518), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 patch-gg.

pixelfx adapts [Sprite Fusion's open-source pixel art VFX](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx),
MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez.

## Everything else

Characters, enemies, vehicles, tiles, backgrounds, items, the font, sound effects and music are original and
generated by the code in `tools/` and `src/game/systems/AudioService.ts`.
